Current delivery: Dongxiaoxue static anatomy migration, 2026-10-17.

All 495 mesh entries from the supplied assembly binary were discarded. The new
binary contains 358 freshly imported official Somakine GLB elements and 137
freshly converted official BodyParts3D OBJ elements. No supplied Human Atlas
optimized mesh bytes are redistributed. The retained task labels and teaching
flow are separate from the newly sourced geometry.

The assembly has 11 body-region chapters, 118 bone definitions and 101 muscle
definitions. Reused structures can occur in more than one chapter; these are
not counts of independent anatomical structures or of the 13-course navigation.

Source manifests, exact official URLs, fixed Somakine commit, source archive
hashes, element IDs, transforms and local SHA-256 digests are recorded in
ASSET_PROVENANCE.json. The source anatomy and these adaptations retain CC BY 4.0.

All geometry uses Y-up metre coordinates. Four zero-area source vertices in
FJ1275 have a deterministic unit Y normal solely for stable lighting; positions
and source triangle indices are unchanged. No new anatomical mirror, measured
joint centre, motion chain or muscle-force model was inferred.

Static rigid placement and geometric reference samples are learning aids.
They are not clinical landmarks, patient-specific attachment coordinates,
clinical joint registration, muscle dynamics, tissue deformation or real gait.

Historical upstream source manifests are preserved as evidence and may describe
the upstream pack's own coverage and transformations. Their counts do not
describe this application. See GEOMETRY_VERIFICATION.json and
COORDINATE_VERIFICATION.json for this build's executable checks.
